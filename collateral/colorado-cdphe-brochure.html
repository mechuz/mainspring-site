What we do

Five things, done by people who have run buildings.

    Take over and stabilize

    A licensed administrator and a director of nursing in the building within days. Residents safe first.

  2. 2

    Fix what the survey found

How it works

Four stages. The building sets the pace.

  1. First 30 days

    Take control

    Leadership in the building. Residents safe. Money under control. A full picture of what we are dealing with, and a plan with dates for you.

  2. Next

    Correct

    Survey problems fixed. Staff rebuilt. Vendors and payroll current.

  3. Then

    Rebuild

    Systems that hold without us. Census and cash steady. Options prepared for you.

  4. Exit

    Resolve

    Hand-off to a qualified operator, or a safe closure. Every resident placed, every dollar accounted for.

Daysto have licensed leadership in the building
Monthlyreporting in the Department’s format
Fixedfees agreed in writing before we start

Only the first 30 days have a set length. How long the rest takes depends on the building: its survey history, its staff, its money. We set that timeline with you at the end of the first 30 days and report against it every month.

Colorado law allows the Department to put a temporary manager or receiver into a failing facility, and the receiver must be a licensed nursing home administrator. Our engagement lead is one.
