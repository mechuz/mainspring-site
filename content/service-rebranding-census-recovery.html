When the building is fixed but the reputation isn't.

When communities call us

  • Census lags the turnaround — the community is better than its reputation
  • A renovation or repositioning deserves a relaunch, not a ribbon
  • Reviews and referral-source sentiment still reflect the old ownership or the old problems
  • Inquiries come in, but the pipeline from call to move-in leaks

What the work includes

  • Market and competitor study: rates, occupancy, positioning gaps
  • Reputation audit — reviews, survey-history visibility, referral-source interviews
  • Repositioning strategy: rehabilitate the name, or rename with a plan
  • Identity, messaging, collateral, website, and signage that match the new reality
  • Relaunch sequence: staff first, referral sources second, public last — with a lead pipeline measured weekly

Census recovery is a system, not a campaign.

The launch event fades in a week. What restores occupancy is referral-source confidence, response-time standards on every inquiry, and a community that delivers on the new promise — which is why this work always follows the operational fix, never substitutes for it.
