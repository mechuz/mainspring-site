For state agencies, courts and receivers

When the state has to step in.

Temporary management under an enforcement remedy. Receivership operations. Independent monitoring. Managed closures with every resident placed. Delivered by licensed administrators who run buildings today, with reporting built for the agency from day one.

How mandated engagements run
  • Licensed NHA and DON in the building within days, matched to the state’s licensure
  • First-72-hour resident safety assessment and a written stabilization plan
  • Weekly reporting in the format the agency or the court specifies
  • Independent financial control — cash, payroll, resident trust funds — and an accounting at the end
  • A defined exit: transfer, sale, or orderly closure
  • Fixed fees that fit a state contract or a court order
