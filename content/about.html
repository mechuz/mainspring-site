The name

A mainspring is the small coiled spring that powers an entire watch. It is how we think about the work: restore the source — leadership, systems, financial footing — and the whole community begins to run again.

The standard we hold

The communities we serve are people’s homes.

Residents and families place real trust in the buildings we work in. We measure every engagement by two results — the operational recovery, and the quality of daily life that follows — because in a well-run community, they arrive together.

It is also why we will tell you when we are not the right firm for a situation. An engagement that cannot reach a real result is not worth either side’s money.
