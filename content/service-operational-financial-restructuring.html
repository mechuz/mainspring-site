Rebuild the operating model, and the numbers underneath it.

When costs have outpaced revenue, the fix is rarely a line-item trim. We rebuild the operating model — and align the budget, the labor plan, and the census-to-cash projection behind it — so the community supports itself.

Speak with a partner Our engagement model

When communities call us

  • Margins are eroding and nobody can say precisely where
  • Agency staffing and overtime have become a structural cost, not a bridge
  • Rates haven't kept pace with acuity, or the care-level pricing doesn't hold up
  • Ownership or lenders need a credible forecast, not last year plus three percent

What the work includes

  • P&L forensics: labor cost per resident day, food cost, agency percentage, benchmark gaps
  • Rate study: care-level pricing against actual acuity and the local market
  • The five biggest financial leaks, sized in dollars, with the fix for each
  • An annual budget built with department heads — theirs to own, ours to pressure-test
  • A monthly variance cadence that keeps the budget honest after we leave

The financial work and the operational work are one job.

A budget that ignores how the schedule actually runs fails by March. That's why the same team that rebuilds your numbers walks your building — the labor model, the census plan, and the operating cadence are designed together, or none of them hold.
