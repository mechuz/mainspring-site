Privacy Policy

Mainspring Healthcare Partners, LLC (“Mainspring,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide through our forms: your name, email address, phone number, organization, role, and the details of your inquiry or consultant application. Consultant applications additionally include professional information such as licensure states, experience, and availability.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, evaluate consultant applications, deliver and improve our services, and communicate with you about engagements. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

Form submissions are processed by our website hosting and form-handling providers, and email is processed by our email provider. These vendors process information on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for other purposes.

Protected health information

This website is not intended to collect protected health information (PHI). Please do not include resident names, medical details, or other PHI in website forms. In client engagements, any access to PHI is governed by a separate business associate agreement, not this policy.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry and application information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, then delete it. We use commercially reasonable safeguards, though no internet transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing db@mainspringhcp.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.

Children

This website is intended for business audiences and not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above reflects the latest revision. Questions: db@mainspringhcp.com, Mainspring Healthcare Partners, New York · Memphis.
