When communities call us

  • Occupancy has slid and the response so far has been to buy more leads
  • The website looks fine and converts nothing; the phone rings and goes to voicemail
  • Google shows a rating the building has outgrown, and a review nobody answered
  • Referral sources have drifted to the competitor who calls them back faster
  • A rebrand or repositioning needs to reach the market, not just the lobby

What the work includes

  • A website built to convert — fast, honest, with pricing and availability where families look for them, and tracked end to end
  • Search optimization: local SEO, Google Business Profile, listings and citations, the content that answers what families actually search
  • Reviews and reputation: a response routine, a review-generation system, and the public quality data kept current
  • Social media engagement that shows real life in the building — run on a calendar, in the community's own voice
  • Referral-source marketing: hospital discharge, case managers, physician practices and home health, with the response time to keep them
  • Census building operations: the weekly funnel scorecard, inbound coverage after five, tour-to-deposit follow-up and the move-in experience
  • Brand and repositioning when the name, the look or the story no longer matches the building

Fix the leak before you fund the faucet.

Most communities already receive enough inquiries to fill their open units and lose them between the first call and the move-in. We measure that funnel first, fix response time and conversion, then spend on demand into a building that can convert it.
