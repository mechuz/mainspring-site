Capital that goes where the return is.

When communities call us

  • The physical plant is dating faster than the competition's
  • A renovation is planned and nobody on staff has run one at this scale
  • An acquisition needs a capital plan lenders and investors will believe
  • The community is repositioning — unit mix, memory care conversion, common areas

What the work includes

  • Facility condition assessment, roof to boiler, with remaining-life estimates
  • A phased capital plan: life-safety first, revenue-driving second, cosmetic last
  • Return modeling per project — rate lift, census impact, payback period
  • Scope documents and a competitive three-bid vendor process
  • Owner's-rep oversight: site meetings, draw reviews, punch list to closeout

Ranked by what protects, then what returns.

Lobbies photograph well, but surveys, insurance, and census ride on the unglamorous systems. Our sequencing protects the license first, then invests where residents and referral sources genuinely notice — with the numbers to support every phase in front of a lender or a board.
