Services

Agents that do the work your building never had hands for.

Every community has jobs that fall through the cracks at 6pm on a Friday — the inquiry nobody called back, the review nobody answered, the payroll anomaly nobody caught until the quarter closed. We design and run AI agents that do that work, and we run the same ones inside our own operations.

Speak with a partner Our engagement model

When communities call us

  • Inquiries and referrals come in around the clock and get answered during business hours, sometimes
  • Leadership gets the numbers weeks late, in a format that takes an hour to read
  • Reviews, listings and social sit unanswered for days
  • Payroll, census and agency spend drift for a whole quarter before anyone notices
  • Vendors are pitching 'AI' and nobody in the building can tell what is real

What the work includes

  • An audit of where time and money leak: which tasks are repetitive, rule-based and currently nobody's job
  • Inquiry and referral agents: instant first response, qualification, follow-up sequences, hand-off to a human with the context attached
  • Review and reputation agents: monitoring, drafted responses in the community's voice, escalation of anything serious
  • Operating intelligence: a daily brief to leadership — census, labor, agency, cash, open items — generated from the systems you already have
  • Payroll and compliance monitors: hours against schedules and census, PBJ readiness, anomalies flagged the day they happen
  • Scheduling, reporting and document workflows connected across your EHR, payroll, CRM and calendar
  • Guardrails: what the agent may do alone, what needs a person, how it is logged — and a kill switch

Built by operators, not sold by vendors.

We are not a software company and we do not resell one. The agents we propose are the ones we run in our own communities — referral follow-up, review response, daily payroll and census briefs — so we know what they actually save and where they fail. Resident clinical decisions stay with licensed humans, always; the agents carry the administrative weight around them.
