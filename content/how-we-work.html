How we work

A disciplined engagement, from first read to handback.

Engagements are led by senior operators and organized around defined outcomes — census, staffing, survey readiness, financial footing — with progress reported monthly and fixed fees tied to milestones. You will always know where things stand, and what remains.

The engagement model

1

Take control — the first 30 days

A senior operator in the building. Leadership covered, residents safe, money under control. By day 30 you have the findings sized in dollars and a plan with numbers and dates, scoped and priced.

2

Correct

What is broken gets fixed: the survey response, staffing, vendors, payroll, cash. It takes as long as the building needs, and the plan says how long we expect.

3

Rebuild

Fixed-scope execution billed on milestones: systems, staffing model, budget, referral relationships — with the terms as clear as the work.

4

Hand back, measured

Outcomes reached, documented, and verified on the scorecard. We transition the operation to permanent leadership, ready to run without us.

The terms we work on

Accountability, in writing.

Senior operators — every engagement is led by people who have run communities.

Defined outcomes — targets with numbers and dates, agreed before the work begins.

Fixed, milestone-based fees — the full cost is clear from the start, and tied to progress.

A monthly scorecard — census, labor, survey readiness, cash, reviewed together every month.

A defined end — engagements conclude when the outcome is reached. Only the first 30 days have a set length; the rest depends on the building.

Straight answers

What does an engagement cost?
Fees are fixed, set by scope, and tied to milestones — the full cost is clear before the work begins. We staff every engagement with senior people, and our fees reflect that.
Do you guarantee results?
We put numbers and dates on every engagement and publish a monthly scorecard against them. Regulatory outcomes belong to the agencies alone; performance accountability is ours, in writing.
Who actually shows up?
Senior operators — people who have held the seat themselves, matched to your state's licensure and your community type.
How fast do you move?
We are in the building within days. The first 30 days end with a plan that has numbers and dates. After that, the pace is set by the building, and the plan says what to expect.
Can we engage for the diagnostic alone?
Yes — it stands on its own. Most clients continue into execution because the targets and scope are already set by the end of the week.
Is resident information protected?
Yes. Chart review happens on-site in your systems, findings are de-identified in reports, and we execute a business associate agreement when required.
