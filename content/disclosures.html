Disclosures

Plain statements about what Mainspring is, what it is not, and how we work — so there is no ambiguity for clients, communities, or regulators.

Nature of our services

Mainspring Healthcare Partners, LLC provides management consulting and advisory services to senior living owners and operators. We are not a law firm, accounting firm, medical practice, or licensed healthcare provider, and nothing we publish or deliver constitutes legal, tax, or medical advice.

Clinical boundaries

Our consultants — including nurse consultants — advise, audit, and train. They do not provide or direct resident care during engagements. Where an engagement includes a consultant serving as the licensed administrator of record, that role and the applicable state licensure are identified expressly in the engagement agreement.

Regulatory outcomes

State and federal agencies alone determine survey results, licensure, and enforcement outcomes. Mainspring commits to the quality of its work and evidence — never to a regulatory result.

Our consultant network

Engagements may be delivered by qualified independent contractors from Mainspring's vetted network of executive directors and nurses, working under written agreement with Mainspring and matched to the licensure requirements of the client's state.

Confidentiality and resident privacy

Client engagements operate under confidentiality agreements. Where work involves access to resident records, we execute business associate agreements, access the minimum necessary, and de-identify findings in written reports.

Results and references

We publish client names, testimonials, or engagement results only with written consent. Any industry statistics we cite are drawn from public sources and identified as such.

Equal opportunity

Non-discrimination in services

We provide our services without discrimination on any basis protected by law. The communities we work with are expected to comply with Title VI of the Civil Rights Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act, Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act, Section 1557 of the Affordable Care Act and applicable state law in the care and admission of residents; our work supports that compliance and never substitutes for it.

Referrals and compensation

We do not pay or accept fees, commissions or anything of value for the referral of residents or patients, and our fees are never contingent on admissions, census or payor mix. Engagement fees are fixed and agreed in writing before work begins.
